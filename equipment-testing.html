How much loss is acceptable?

1 dB loss means 79.4% of the signal will get through. This loss relative to no loss at all will generally go unnoticed in most QSOs. Only in very marginal contacts, e.g. EME, could it make a difference.

However, 3 dB is a whole S-point on most rigs and so will be significant on any weak <S3 QSO, with half the signal lost in the feeder.

You should try to keep your feeder loss below 1 dB on all bands by using the appropriate coax. This means if you have 25 metres of feeder you are going to need LDF4-50 or better on 432 MHz.

So, what can you do if this is not practical? Fit a masthead preamp on RX and either increase TX power to compensate for the loss, or have a masthead power amp.
