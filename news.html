Upcoming events

Wednesday the 18th March Scout Building Ballasalla Demonstration of JS8Call A weak signal keyboard-to-keyboard messaging system based on FT8 which can operate with a signal to noise ratio of -24dB as measured in a 2.5kHz bandwidth. The software needed is free and can run on Windows, MacOS and Linux.

Thursday the 26th March Creg-ny-Baa Hotel Talk How to have QSOs by bouncing signals off planes, meteors, and the moon. By Richard GD8EXI Starting 19:30 local time in the upstairs room.

Wednesday 1st April Scout Building Ballasalla Demonstration of FreeDV FreeDV is a suite of digital voice modes for HF radio. The flagship mode is the Radio Autoencoder (RADE). You can run RADE using a free GUI application for Windows, Linux and macOS that allows any SSB radio to be used for high quality digital voice.

Bring and Buy Sale

February 18, 2026

On Wednesday the 18th of February we are going to run a Bring and Buy Sale at the Scout Building starting at 19:30 GMT, where you can bring along your own equipment to sell.

If you have items likely to be of particular interest, please post details on the club’s Facebook page to raise interest or send details to info@IOMARS.IM so a committee member can post it for you.

The club has a dustbin full of coax mostly fitted with plugs to sell along with some ready-made dipoles, new plugs and some small transformers. There will also be some PSUs, AMUs, and RF Powermeters for sale at bargain prices. Many low value items will be displayed on tables each marked with a price per item e.g. £2.00, 5.00, 10.00 and 20.00.

— IOMARS Admin

Testing Night Wednesday 21st January at the Scout Building in Ballasalla 19:00 Start

January 21, 2026

This testing night will have an antenna and feeder theme. We are going to try to turn the scout hall into a testing range for antennas from 144MHz up to 3.5GHz. No guarantee it will work, and the figures will only be relative, but it should still be interesting. The equipment will also allow us to measure the loss in coax plotted against frequency and the return loss curve for the antennas. So please bring along any antennas and feeders you want testing with plugs already fitted. If you have your own test equipment, then please feel free to bring that along for comparison purposes. There will be some discussion about what the results mean in the real world.

Time permitting other testing will also be possible like harmonic and power output. Filter insertion loss and amplifier gain all plotted against frequency.

2025 AGM

December 12, 2025

As discussed at the AGM we are making some changes to IOMARS meeting format for 2026.


1. The Scout Building meetings in Ballasalla will only take place on the 1st and 3rd Wednesday of each month.
2. The Creg-ny-Baa meetings will still be on the last Thursday of the month except in May and August when the dates will be changed to make way for the TT and Manx GP.
3. We will have testing nights, demonstrations and bring and buy sales at the Scout Building however talks will normally be at the Creg on the last Thursday of the month.

The last IOMARS meeting of 2025 will be on 17th December at the Scout building, with mince pies.

 

Not surprisingly no Wednesday meetings on either Christmas Eve or New Year’s Eve.

The first meeting of 2026 will be on the 7thJanuary at the Scout Building, followed by one on the 21st. The first Creg meeting will be on the 29th of January.
