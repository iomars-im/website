On Wednesday the 18th of February we are going to run a Bring and Buy Sale at the Scout Building starting at 19:30 GMT, where you can bring along your own equipment to sell. 

If you have items likely to be of particular interest, please post details on the club’s Facebook page to raise interest or send details to info@IOMARS.IM so a committee member can post it for you.


The club has a dustbin full of coax mostly fitted with plugs to sell along with some ready-made dipoles, new plugs and some small transformers. There will also be some PSUs, AMUs, and RF Powermeters for sale at bargain prices.
Many low value items will be displayed on tables each marked with a price per item e.g. £2.00, 5.00, 10.00 and 20.00.
