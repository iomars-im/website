Equipment to Loan

Try before you buy - Club members can borrow equipment at no cost

Club members have the opportunity to borrow equipment from the Club before taking the plunge themselves. This allows you to gain hands-on experience and ensure equipment meets your needs before making a purchase.

Speak to a committee member at a club meeting, or email info@iomars.im for more information about equipment availability and loan terms.

Available Equipment Types:

  • HF radios
  • VHF/UHF radios
  • DMR equipment
  • Power supplies
  • Antennas and accessories
  • Other amateur radio equipment

Club Station

The club maintains a station at our meeting location where members can operate and gain experience with various modes and frequencies. Equipment is available during club meetings for members to use.

Equipment for Sale

Club equipment available for purchase by members

From time to time, the club has equipment available for sale to members. This may include items donated to the club or upgraded equipment being replaced.

How to Purchase

  1. Check for available equipment below
  2. Equipment listings will be announced to members via email
  3. Contact the listed person to arrange viewing and purchase
  4. All proceeds support club activities and equipment maintenance
